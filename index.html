HOW IT WORKS

1. THE NUMBERS

Play-by-play EPA, recent form, injuries, weather and rest all feed a team rating, refreshed every Tuesday.

2. 5,000 SIMS

Each game is played out 5,000 times. The model's score is the typical result, the win % is how often each side came out on top.

3. MY PICKS

Then I pick every game myself. Sometimes I side with the machine, sometimes I back my gut. Both get graded.

4. WATCHABILITY

Quality, how close it should be and expected points, adjusted for UK kick-off times. From “Clear the sofa” down to “Record it”.
